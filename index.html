About Whimsy Touch

the table of your dreams, borrowed, not bought

Whimsy Touch is a tablescape rental in Davao City. We lend you the pieces, and you set the table yourself, wherever you’re celebrating.

You don’t need a stylist to style your affair,
just borrow the pieces and place them with care —
we bring the runners, the candlelight, the bloom,
you make the magic happen in your own room.

What we bring

  • A table runner in the color you choose
  • LED candles, pre-loaded with batteries
  • Candle holders and clear vases
  • Fresh flowers, placemats & napkins, with Full Bloom
  • A care card to guide your setup

What you bring

  • Your venue, home or restaurant
  • The food and drinks
  • Plates, glasses and cutlery
  • Your table, and about 30 minutes to set it up

We’re not a restaurant, caterer or venue, just the pieces that make your table unforgettable. Setup isn’t included, but with Full Bloom you can add our Fairy Godmother Service and we’ll set it up for you. Every rental is for 24 hours, starting when you receive the items.
