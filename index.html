FAQs

Good to know

Is setup included?

Our tablescapes are DIY rentals: we deliver everything with an easy guide. Want us to do it? Add our Fairy Godmother Service with Full Bloom or Calla Lily Dream.

How much is delivery?

Our rider delivers and picks up at the Grab rate, paid directly to the rider before each trip.

How long is the rental?

24 hours, starting when you receive the items.

How do I reserve my date?

Book at thewhimsytouch.com and pay a 50% downpayment. The balance and refundable deposit are due 1 day before your event.
