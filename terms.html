Rental Terms & Conditions

Sending a booking through our website means you agree to these terms.

1. Payment

  • Package prices do not include the security deposit or delivery fee.
  • A booking is confirmed only once the downpayment is received in our account. A receipt screenshot alone does not confirm a booking.
  • Runners are held while a payment is being verified. If the payment cannot be verified, the booking is cancelled.
  • The remaining balance is due 1 day before the event.

2. Security Deposit

  • A refundable security deposit is required: ₱300 Storybook; ₱500 Once Upon and Ever After; ₱500 Storybook with Full Bloom; ₱600 Once Upon with Full Bloom.
  • Items are counted and photographed with a timestamp when released and when returned.
  • The deposit is refunded after the items are checked, less the cost of any damaged, missing or unreturned items.
  • The renter pays any amount above the deposit.
  • Refunds are sent to the account the renter confirms after the event.

3. Rental Period, Delivery & Pickup

  • The rental period is 24 hours from when the renter receives the items. Items are delivered no earlier than 24 hours before the event.
  • Deliveries and pickups are done by our rider. If our rider is unavailable, we will book a Grab or Maxim rider.
  • Delivery fees follow the Grab app rate and are paid to the rider before each trip. Items are released only once paid.
  • Delivery fees are separate from the security deposit and are never deducted from it.
  • The time given when booking is the delivery arrival time.
  • Items must be ready for pickup by the end of the rental period. We may schedule an earlier pickup, with notice, if the items are booked right after.
  • Late returns may be deducted from the deposit.

4. Use & Care

  • Items may only be used for the booked event and must be returned in the same condition.
  • Renters who use their own flame candles are responsible for fire safety. Runners and holders must be returned free of wax.

5. Damage & Loss

  • The renter is responsible for all items from delivery until they are returned and checked.
  • Damaged, broken, stained or missing items are charged at replacement cost, taken from the deposit first.

6. Cancellation & Rescheduling

  • Cancelled more than 3 days before the event: full refund of the amount paid.
  • Cancelled within 3 days of the event: 50% refund of the amount paid.
  • Full Bloom with fresh flowers: cancelled 1 day before or on the event day: no refund, as the flowers are already bought. Only the security deposit is returned.
  • Rescheduling has no penalty and depends on the new date being available.

7. Fairy Godmother Service

  • The fee covers setup and pack-down only. It does not add items to the booking.
  • Delivery is free within Roxas–Lanang–Indangan. Beyond that it is ₱25 per km each way, up to 20 km.
  • Pack-down from 11:00 PM to 1:30 AM has a ₱300 late-night fee, included in the booking total. There is no pack-down after 1:30 AM.
  • For next-day pickup (from 8:00 AM), we leave our boxes with the renter. The renter must switch off all candles, pack the items in the boxes, send photos, and keep the items safe until collected. Free within Roxas–Lanang–Indangan; the per-km rate applies beyond.
  • Leaving items at the venue overnight is not advisable. If items are left there, they remain the renter’s responsibility.
