Rental Terms & Conditions

Sending a booking through our website means you agree to these terms.

1. Payment

  • Listed package prices exclude the security deposit and delivery fee.
  • A downpayment confirms your booking and secures your date.
  • Payment details (MariBank / InstaPay QR) are shown right after you send your booking, where you can also upload your receipt.
  • Your booking is confirmed once the payment shows in our account (a receipt screenshot alone doesn’t confirm it). We’ll then email your invoice.
  • Any remaining balance is due 1 day before the event date.
  • Once you upload your receipt, we hold your runner while we check it. If the payment can’t be verified, the booking is cancelled.

2. Security Deposit

  • Security deposit (separate from the package price): ₱300 Storybook, ₱500 Once Upon and Ever After. With Full Bloom: ₱500 Storybook, ₱600 Once Upon.
  • Items are counted (with photos) before they leave, and again when they’re returned.
  • The deposit is refunded after items are checked back in, minus the cost of any damaged, missing, or unreturned pieces.
  • If damage or loss exceeds the deposit amount, the renter agrees to cover the difference.
  • Refunds go to the account you confirm with us after the event, not automatically the one you paid from.

3. Delivery & Pickup

  • Every rental is for 24 hours, starting when you receive the items. Items are delivered no more than 24 hours before your event.
  • Delivery and pickup are by our own rider, required for every booking (except Fairy Godmother Service) so your items are handled with care. No need to book anything yourself.
  • Delivery fee follows the Grab app rate for your location, so you can check it anytime. We use our own trusted rider so your pieces arrive in good condition.
  • Paying the rider: before each trip (drop-off and pickup), the rider sends the fare and their QR code. Pay before they head out, or have the exact cash ready. Items are handed over once paid.
  • The delivery fee is separate from your security deposit. It's paid to the rider and is never deducted from the deposit.
  • The time you enter when booking is your delivery arrival time, when the items reach your venue. Leave enough time to set up.
  • Our rider will send you a Google Maps live location link on the day, so you can follow your items on the way.
  • With Fairy Godmother Service: free delivery within Roxas–Lanang–Indangan, then ₱25 per km each way past it, up to 20 km. Farther than that, please message us first.
  • Pickup can be from a different place than your venue (like your home); just add its Google Maps link when booking.
  • Items must be ready for pickup within 24 hours of receiving them (e.g. received 7:00 PM, due back 7:00 PM the next day).
  • If the items are booked right after you, we may schedule an earlier pickup. We’ll tell you as soon as we know.
  • Your preferred pickup (same day after the event, or the next day) and pickup time are given when booking.
  • Renters needing more time should notify Whimsy Touch as early as possible — late returns may affect deposit refunds.

4. Use & Care of Items

  • Our candles are flameless LED. If you use your own flame candles, you’re responsible for fire safety.
  • No wax drippings are allowed on the runners. Any flame candles used must be returned with the runner and holders completely free of wax.
  • All rented pieces should be handled with care; vases and candle holders are fragile.
  • Items must be used only for the booked event and returned in the same condition they were provided.

5. Damages & Loss

  • The renter is responsible for all items from delivery until they are returned to and checked by Whimsy Touch.
  • Damaged, broken, stained, or missing items are charged at replacement cost, deducted from the security deposit first.

6. Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • Cancellations or rescheduling requests should be made as early as possible so the date can be released or moved for other clients.
  • Canceled more than 3 days before the event: full refund of any amount paid.
  • Canceled within 3 days of the event: 50% of the total amount paid is refunded.
  • Rescheduling instead of canceling avoids this penalty and is subject to availability of the new date.

7. Fairy Godmother Service

  • Not available October 29 to November 5.
  • Available only with Full Bloom on the same booking.
  • The rate covers our time only (setting up and packing away). It doesn’t add items to your booking.
  • Includes delivery within Roxas, Lanang, Buhangin and Cabantian up to Indangan. Farther venues have a delivery fee (see Delivery & Pickup).
  • The service includes setup and packing down at the venue after your event.
  • Late-night pack-down (11:00 PM to 1:30 AM): +₱300. We don’t pack down after 1:30 AM; later events use next-day pickup.
  • The late-night fee is based on when we actually finish, even if an earlier time was given. It’s paid the same night, or deducted from your deposit.
  • Next-day pickup: pack the items into our boxes after the event and we’ll collect them the next day from 8:00 AM. Free within Roxas–Lanang–Indangan; farther follows the delivery rates above.
  • With next-day pickup, switch off every candle, send us photos of the packed items, and keep them safe until we collect them.
  • Leaving items at the venue overnight? Arrange it with the venue, give them our contact, and tell us their opening time. If the items are booked before the venue opens, bring them home and we’ll pick up there.

8. Runner Layering

  • You can layer any two colors that are available for your date and Gathering.
  • Both colors are reserved as soon as you book, and the layering fee is part of your downpayment.
