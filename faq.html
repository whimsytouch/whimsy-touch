FAQs

Got questions? We’ve got answers!

Here’s everything you need to know before booking your Gathering.

Your table

What exactly is Whimsy Touch?

We’re a DIY tablescape rental. We lend you the table décor (runner, LED candles, candle holders and vases) and you set it up yourself at your venue or home.

We’re not a restaurant, caterer or venue, so food, drinks and the venue aren’t included.

Is food or a venue included?

No, we only rent the table décor. You bring the venue (a restaurant, event space or your own home), the food and drinks, and the plates, glasses and cutlery.

What does each Gathering look like?

Every Gathering shares the same lovely look, we just adjust the guest count, item quantity, and runner length for you.

You choose the color and any little upgrades to make it even more magical.

Is it still the same rate even if we’re only 6 pax?

Yes, our rates are set per Gathering, not per exact guest count, so as long as it fits within your chosen Gathering’s guest range, the price stays the same.

Can I mix or layer two runner colors?

Yes! You can layer any two colors, as long as both are available for your Gathering. It’s +₱50 per runner position (+₱50 Sweetheart & Storybook, +₱100 Once Upon, +₱150 Ever After).

Are flowers included?

The standard setup comes with clear acrylic vases, but flowers aren’t included. Want fresh blooms? Add Full Bloom: fresh flowers from local vendors, never artificial.

Tableware (plates, glasses and cutlery) isn’t included, even with Full Bloom.

Are the candles real flame?

No, all our candles are battery-powered LED, so there’s no wax, no smoke and no venue restrictions. They come pre-loaded, with spare batteries included.

Do you rent tablecloths?

We only rent table runners, not tablecloths. Our runners look lovely on a bare wooden or plain table.

Can I rent just a few pieces?

Yes! A gold cake stand, candles, candle holders and glass vases are available à la carte, no Gathering needed.

I have an inspiration photo, can you recreate it?

Our pieces come in set styles, so we can’t build something fully custom from scratch.

Add your own personal touches, or enjoy it lovely as is. With our range of colored runners, we can already help you achieve the mood you’re after.

Setup

Is setup included?

Whimsy Touch is a DIY tablescape rental, so setup isn’t included, but our care card is there to guide you every step of the way.

If you’ve booked Full Bloom, you can add our Fairy Godmother Service for a helping hand.

How long does it take to set up?

Setup takes less than 30 minutes, quick and easy so you can spend more time enjoying your celebration.

Why is Fairy Godmother Service only with Full Bloom?

Full Bloom comes with more pieces and takes a bit more time to set up, so it’s the only upgrade where you can add Fairy Godmother Service and let us handle the whole setup for you.

Which areas does Fairy Godmother Service cover?

The rate covers venues from Lanang to Roxas (downtown Davao). Farther venues: ₱250 each way up to 10 km past, ₱300 each way up to 20 km past. Farther than that, please message us first.

Booking & payment

How do I book?

Simply fill out our booking form to reserve your date.

A 50% downpayment locks it in, with the remaining balance and deposit due the day before your celebration.

Is my date booked once I send the form?

Not yet. Right after you send the form, you’ll see our payment details and can upload your receipt. Your date is secured once we confirm your downpayment in our account, and we’ll email your invoice.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Canceled more than 3 days before your event: full refund. Canceled within 3 days: 50% of the amount paid is refunded. Rescheduling instead avoids this, subject to availability on the new date.

Deposit & care

Is there a security deposit?

Yes, a small deposit that’s separate from your price, and it’s returned to you once everything comes back safe and sound: ₱300 for Sweetheart & Storybook, ₱500 for Once Upon & Ever After, ₱200 for à la carte orders. With Full Bloom, it’s ₱350 for Sweetheart, ₱500 for Storybook and ₱600 for Once Upon, since there are more pieces.

How do I get my deposit back?

Once everything is checked back in, we send it to the GCash or bank account you confirm with us after the event. We’ll always ask; we don’t assume it’s the account you paid from.

What if something breaks or goes missing?

Accidents happen! Damaged or missing pieces are charged at replacement cost, taken from your deposit first. If it’s more than the deposit, you cover the difference.

Anything to remember before returning?

Please switch off every candle, keep everything dry, and wipe off any wax if you used your own candles. You can leave the pieces inside the insulated bag.

Delivery & return

Where are you located?

We’re based in Davao City, Philippines, and happy to deliver anywhere in the city for your special day!

How does delivery work?

Delivery and pickup are by our own rider. The fee follows the Maxim rate for your location and is paid directly to the rider before each trip (one fee for drop-off, one for pickup). It's separate from your security deposit and is never deducted from it.

With Fairy Godmother Service, delivery is free within Lanang to Roxas, since we come to set up your table.

How long is the rental?

Every rental is for 24 hours, starting when you receive the items. Please have everything ready for pickup or return by then.

If you need more time, message us as early as possible and we’ll check if the items are free.

Still have a question?

Message us anytime, or build your table and see your price.
