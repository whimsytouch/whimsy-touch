Rental Terms & Conditions

Sending a booking through our website means you agree to these terms.

1. Payment

  • Listed package prices exclude the security deposit and delivery fee.
  • A downpayment confirms your booking and secures your date.
  • Payment details (MariBank / InstaPay QR) are shown right after you send your booking, where you can also upload your receipt.
  • Your booking is confirmed only once we see the payment in our account. A receipt screenshot alone does not confirm a booking. Your invoice is emailed to you after we confirm.
  • Any remaining balance is due 1 day before the event date.

2. Security Deposit

  • Every booking carries a security deposit, separate from the package price: ₱300 for Sweetheart and Storybook, ₱500 for Once Upon and Ever After. With Full Bloom: ₱350 Sweetheart, ₱500 Storybook, ₱600 Once Upon. À la carte orders: ₱200.
  • Items are counted (with photos) before they leave, and again when they’re returned.
  • The deposit is refunded after items are checked back in, minus the cost of any damaged, missing, or unreturned pieces.
  • If damage or loss exceeds the deposit amount, the renter agrees to cover the difference.
  • Refunds are sent to a payout account confirmed with the renter after the event — this is not assumed to be the same account the booking was paid from.

3. Delivery & Pickup

  • Items are delivered no more than 24 hours before the event.
  • Standard and Fairy Dust bookings, and Sweetheart with Full Bloom: delivery and return are arranged on the day, and the renter pays the courier directly (COD or as agreed). Whimsy Touch is not liable for damage occurring in transit once items are handed over.
  • Storybook and Once Upon with Full Bloom: since it’s a big setup that doesn’t fit on a motorbike, Whimsy Touch delivers and picks up by car. ₱100 each way within Buhangin, ₱200 each way within Lanang to Roxas, ₱250 each way up to 10 km past Lanang to Roxas, ₱300 each way up to 20 km past. Farther than that, please message us before booking.
  • With Fairy Godmother Service, delivery is free within Lanang to Roxas. Farther venues: ₱250 each way up to 10 km past Lanang to Roxas, ₱300 each way up to 20 km past. Farther than that, please message us before booking.
  • Drop-off and pickup are priced separately, so pickup can be from a different place (like your home). Distance is measured by road from the nearest edge of our delivery area. Delivery fees are computed from your venue on the booking page and paid with your booking. If the location turns out to be different, we’ll let you know before your event.
  • Return items within 24 hours after the event ends. If the same items are booked for another gathering the same day, this window may be shortened to as little as 3 hours after the event ends — Whimsy Touch will confirm the exact return time with the renter in advance.
  • Renters needing more time should notify Whimsy Touch as early as possible — late returns may affect deposit refunds.

4. Use & Care of Items

  • Standard candles provided are flameless LED. If the renter uses their own flame candles instead, they are responsible for fire safety and must keep them away from anything flammable.
  • No wax drippings are allowed on the runners. Any flame candles used must be returned with the runner and holders completely free of wax.
  • All rented pieces should be handled with care; vases and candle holders are fragile.
  • Items must be used only for the booked event and returned in the same condition they were provided.

5. Damages & Loss

  • The renter is responsible for all items from delivery until they are returned to and checked by Whimsy Touch.
  • Damaged, broken, stained, or missing items are charged at replacement cost, deducted from the security deposit first.

6. Cancellations & Rescheduling

  • Cancellations or rescheduling requests should be made as early as possible so the date can be released or moved for other clients.
  • Canceled more than 3 days before the event: full refund of any amount paid.
  • Canceled within 3 days of the event: 50% of the total amount paid is refunded.
  • Rescheduling instead of canceling avoids this penalty and is subject to availability of the new date.

7. Fairy Godmother Service

  • Available only with Full Bloom on the same booking.
  • The rate covers venues from Lanang to Roxas (downtown Davao). Farther venues are charged a delivery fee (see Delivery & Pickup).

8. Runner Layering

  • Pink and White are always available for layering. Other second colors are subject to availability, and we’ll confirm them 1 day before your event.
  • With Pink or White, the layering fee is included in your downpayment. With other colors, it’s not part of your downpayment and is added to your remaining balance once your second color is confirmed.
  • If your second color is no longer available, we’ll suggest the closest match. If you’d rather skip layering, you won’t be charged the layering fee.

Wishing your day a touch of whimsy!
